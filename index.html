About

Driving Pharmaceutical Excellence

A results-driven leader in clinical affairs and retail pharmacy operations

Dr. Dilmini Udugampola is an experienced Manager in Clinical Affairs with a demonstrated history of working in the retail industry. Currently serving as Deputy General Manager - Pharma Sector at Cargills Ceylon, she heads the Cargills Food City pharmacy chain including Procurement & Supply Chain, Management & Regulations.

With extensive experience spanning both Sri Lanka and the United Kingdom, Dr. Udugampola brings a unique blend of clinical expertise, commercial acumen, and strategic leadership to pharmaceutical retail operations. Her multifaceted educational background combines traditional pharmacy with alternative medicine, supply chain management, psychology & counseling, and business administration.
